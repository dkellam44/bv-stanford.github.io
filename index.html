🏢 The Reality of Hotel Data

Hotel data is notoriously trapped across 10+ disconnected SaaS vendor logins. AI models cannot automate or optimize what they cannot read.

  • Structured: ADR, RevPAR curves, food & beverage POS check sizes, OTA net payout feeds.
  • Unstructured: Operations manuals, brand standards, guest sentiment reviews, staff notes.
⚡ Punching Above Mid-Market Weight

Stanford Hotels (13 properties, ~4,046 rooms) does not need a 20-person data engineering department to compete with global hotel chains.

  • In-House Agility: Build lightweight connectors and clean schemas using modern coding agents and Python.
  • No Vendor Trap: Avoid paying $20K/mo retainers for bloated MarTech SaaS UIs; own the raw data layer directly.
🛡️ House Data Governance & Safety

Enterprise data expansion must adhere to strict security and guest privacy guardrails.

  • Data Sovereignty: Stanford owns 100% of raw guest event logs and schemas—never held hostage by agencies.
  • PII Masking: Automatic redaction of guest phone numbers and payment data prior to LLM analysis.
  • Deterministic Gates: AI drafts campaigns and audits; humans approve executions.

Strategic Pillar 3 · Margin Defense

OTA Interception & Revenue Management

Driving Shane's ~7% direct booking target against 15%–25% OTA commission leakage

Direct Margin Optimization
⚖️ Legally Bypassing Rate Parity

OTAs contractually ban cheaper public rates on vanity sites. Beat them through fenced channels:

  • Closed User Group: 1-click email/social gating makes discounts private member rates (100% exempt from parity).
  • "Shadow Discounts": Bundle high-perceived-value / low-marginal-cost perks ($65 free valet parking, $25 Cavaña credit, 1:00 PM late checkout).
📱 Eliminating Booking Friction

50%+ of travelers bounce back to Expedia because their credit card is already saved.

  • 1-Click Apple / Google Pay: Native mobile tokens eliminate typing 16 credit card digits into clunky iframes.
  • 15-Min Abandonment Magic Links: Automated SMS/email re-opens their exact dates and room with perks pre-applied.
🔄 Post-Stay Retention

Acquiring a guest on Expedia once is CAC. Paying Expedia twice for the same guest is operational failure.

  • Real Email Capture: Front desk + Wi-Fi captive portals bypass Expedia's proxy email address.
  • VIP Direct Re-engagement: Revinate delivers exclusive lifetime direct re-booking codes (LUMADIRECT).
