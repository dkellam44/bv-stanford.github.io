🔌 OPERA & Brand OXI Gateway

Treats PMS/CRS as authoritative systems of record. Communicates via audited OXI gateways or HTNG webhooks, ensuring zero direct DB lockups.

📶 Wi-Fi Margin Bridge

ElevenOS captive portal auth captures verified guest identity from OTA bookings, injecting real emails into Revinate for future direct marketing.

🛡️ Zero-Shadow-IT Compliance

All marketing tags and pipelines federate via Entra ID SSO with strict PII masking before touching any analytics or AI systems.

⚖️ Daily Reconciliation Engine

Reconciling GA4 cross-domain checkout events against PMS room nights nightly prevents attributing phantom revenue to ad spend.

🍽️ POS to Folio Posting

Simphony POS queries OPERA via FIAS/HTNG to validate room status before posting dining charges to the guest ledger.

🔄 Direct Re-engagement

Captured Wi-Fi profiles feed automated win-back campaigns in Revinate, driving repeat visits directly through vanity booking engines.
